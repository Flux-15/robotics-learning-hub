# Getting Support with the Robotics Learning Hub

Welcome! We are excited to help you on your robotics engineering journey.

## 💬 Community & Discussion

* **Questions & Conceptual Clarifications:** If you have questions about a robotics concept (e.g., how to tune PID, understanding Lie groups, or setting up ROS 2), please open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/Flux-15/robotics-learning-hub/discussions) or join our community threads.
* **Bug Reports & Broken Links:** If you find a typo, dead link, or broken code snippet, please [Open an Issue](https://github.com/Flux-15/robotics-learning-hub/issues).
* **Hardware & Project Showcase:** Built one of the rovers, robotic arms, or drone projects? Share your build in the Discussions showcase tab!

## 🤝 Contributing
Want to contribute code, tutorials, or papers? Read our **[Contribution Guide](CONTRIBUTING.md)** and check out **[Good First Issues](https://github.com/Flux-15/robotics-learning-hub/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22)**!

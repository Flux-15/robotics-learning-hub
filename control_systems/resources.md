> [!TIP]
> For the comprehensive engineering manual covering PID anti-windup, state-space representations, LQR Riccati equations, Model Predictive Control (MPC), and Kalman Filters (EKF/UKF), see the **[Control Systems Guide](README.md)**.

## Overview
Control systems ensure a robot behaves accurately and stably despite physical disturbances, modeling inaccuracies, and sensor noise. This covers PID control, state-space models, LQR (Linear Quadratic Regulators), MPC (Model Predictive Control), and state estimation.

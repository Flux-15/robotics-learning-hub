## Overview
Training and validating robotic intelligence requires data bridging visual perception, continuous action trajectories, sensor noise, and multimodal interactions.

## Major Autonomous Driving and Localization Datasets
- [KITTI Vision Benchmark Suite](http://www.cvlibs.net/datasets/kitti/) — Foundational benchmark for stereo, optical flow, visual odometry, and 3D object detection.
- [NuScenes (by Motional)](https://www.nuscenes.org/) — Full 360-degree multimodal dataset with 6 cameras, 1 LiDAR, and 5 radar sensors.
- [Waymo Open Dataset](https://waymo.com/open/) — High-quality multi-sensor autonomous driving dataset across diverse weather.
- [Argoverse 2](https://www.argoverse.org/) — Next-generation dataset for 3D tracking, motion forecasting, and LiDAR perception.

## Embodied AI & Robotic Manipulation Datasets
- [Open X-Embodiment (OXE / RT-X)](https://robotics-transformer-x.github.io/) — 1M+ demonstration episodes across 22 robot embodiments.
- [DROID Dataset](https://droid-dataset.github.io/) — 76k real-world manipulation trajectories across 564 environments.
- [BridgeData v2](https://rail-berkeley.github.io/bridgedata/) — Large-scale dataset for robotic manipulation in home/kitchen environments.
- [DexNet (Berkeley Automation)](https://berkeleyautomation.github.io/dex-net/) — 6.7M synthetic point clouds and parallel-jaw grasps.
- [RoboNet Wiki](https://www.robonet.wiki/) — Multi-robot manipulation video dataset.

## Visual Odometry, SLAM & 3D Spatial Benchmarks
- [EuRoC MAV Dataset](https://projects.asl.ethz.ch/datasets/doku.php?id=kmav-visual-inertial-datasets) — Synchronized stereo cameras and high-rate IMU on a drone with ground truth.
- [TUM RGB-D Benchmark](https://vision.in.tum.de/data/datasets/rgbd-dataset) — Ground truth RGB-D trajectories for evaluating camera drift.
- [Replica Dataset](https://github.com/facebookresearch/Replica-Dataset) — Photorealistic 3D indoor reconstructed environments.
- [ScanNet](http://www.scan-net.org/) — 2.5M RGB-D indoor frames with 3D camera poses.

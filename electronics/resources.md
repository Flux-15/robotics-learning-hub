# ⚡ Electronics & Power Systems Resources

> [!TIP]
> For the comprehensive engineering manual on power distribution, FOC motor control, LiPo battery thresholds, and KiCad PCB design, see the **[Electronics Guide](README.md)**.

## Overview
Robots require robust power regulation and hardware interfacing. This domain covers circuit design, power systems (batteries, BMS, switching converters), sensor integration, motor drivers, and noise protection.

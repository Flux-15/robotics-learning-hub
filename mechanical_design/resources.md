# ⚙️ Mechanical Design & Actuation Resources

> [!TIP]
> For the comprehensive engineering manual on parametric 3D CAD, actuator sizing, QDD vs Harmonic gearboxes, reflected inertia, and PA-CF fabrication, see the **[Mechanical Design Guide](README.md)**.

## Overview
The physical embodiment of a robot. This domain encompasses 3D modeling (CAD), kinematics analysis, materials selection, structural integrity, and choosing actuators (servos, BLDCs, steppers, planetary/strain wave drives).

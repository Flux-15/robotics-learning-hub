# FastSLAM: A Factored Solution to the Simultaneous Localization and Mapping Problem

**Authors:** Michael Montemerlo, Sebastian Thrun, Daphne Koller, Ben Wegbreit

**Publication:** AAAI National Conference on Artificial Intelligence (2002)

**Paper Link:** [FastSLAM: A Factored Solution to the Simultaneous Localization and Mapping Problem](https://www.aaai.org/Papers/AAAI/2002/AAAI02-089.pdf)

## Summary
FastSLAM outlines a method to solve the SLAM problem using a Rao-Blackwellized particle filter, effectively decoupling the robot pose estimation from the landmark location estimation.

## Applications
Indoor mobile robots, probabilistic mapping, autonomous exploration.

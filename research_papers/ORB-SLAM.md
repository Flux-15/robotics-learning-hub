# ORB-SLAM: A Versatile and Accurate Monocular SLAM System

**Authors:** Raul Mur-Artal, J. M. M. Montiel, Juan D. Tardos

**Publication:** IEEE Transactions on Robotics (2015)

**Paper Link:** [ORB-SLAM: A Versatile and Accurate Monocular SLAM System](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.00956)

## Summary
This paper presents ORB-SLAM, a reliable and feature-based monocular SLAM system that operates in real-time. It covers tracking, mapping, relocalization, and loop closing using ORB features.

## Applications
Autonomous navigation, augmented reality, mobile robotics.

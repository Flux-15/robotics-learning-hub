## Overview
Robotics moves at the speed of academia. Tracking the progression from early path planning heuristics to 3D Gaussian Splatting and multimodal Vision-Language-Action foundation models is essential for modern engineers.

## Key Conferences and Journals

- **RSS** (Robotics: Science and Systems)

- **IJRR** (The International Journal of Robotics Research)

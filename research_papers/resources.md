# Robotics Research Papers 

## Overview
Robotics moves at the speed of academia. Tracking the progression from SLAM optimization to end-to-end deep reinforcement learning is crucial.

## Key Conferences and Journals
- **ICRA** (International Conference on Robotics and Automation)
- **IROS** (Intelligent Robots and Systems)
- **RSS** (Robotics: Science and Systems)
- **CoRL** (Conference on Robot Learning)
- **TRO** (IEEE Transactions on Robotics)
- **IJRR** (The International Journal of Robotics Research)

## Navigation and SLAM Foundation Papers
- *ORB-SLAM: A Versatile and Accurate Monocular SLAM System* (Raul Mur-Artal et al., 2015) - [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.00956)
- *FastSLAM: A Factored Solution to the Simultaneous Localization and Mapping Problem* (Montemerlo et al., 2002) - [Link](https://www.aaai.org/Papers/AAAI/2002/AAAI02-089.pdf)
- *LOAM: Lidar Odometry and Mapping in Real-time* (Zhang and Singh, 2014) - [Link](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/pub_files/2014/7/Ji_LidarMapping_RSS2014_v8.pdf)

## Motion Planning Papers
- *Rapidly-Exploring Random Trees: A New Tool for Path Planning* (LaValle, 1998)
- *A Formal Basis for the Heuristic Determination of Minimum Cost Paths* (Hart, Nilsson, Raphael, 1968 - **A* pathfinding**)
- *CHOMP: Covariant Hamiltonian Optimization for Motion Planning* (Ratliff et al., 2009)

## Deep Learning in Robotics
- *End-to-End Training of Deep Visuomotor Policies* (Levine et al., 2016)
- *QT-Opt: Scalable Deep Reinforcement Learning for Vision-Based Robotic Manipulation* (Kalashnikov et al., 2018)
- *RT-1: Robotics Transformer for Real-World Control at Scale* (Brohan et al., 2022)
